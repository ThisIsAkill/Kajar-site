Battle engine

Bank $C1 is the battle engine's master bank. This page holds its shared services: the entry vectors and service dispatch, hardware multiply and divide, the shift helpers, trig and geometry, and small battle-system predicates and panel helpers.

See also: Battle menu, Battle targeting.
