Engine core

Bank $C0's core: the one-time startup and the per-frame GameLoop, VBlank handling, location loading and scene save/restore, the per-frame state machine with its mode handlers (the door and switch tile animations), and the sprite-slot init system that stages OAM data and streams sprite tiles to VRAM.

Routines still labelled Sub_xxxx are matched byte-for-byte but not yet named: they are listed under "Engine (unnamed)".

See also: Sprite Init Explainer, ROM Map.
